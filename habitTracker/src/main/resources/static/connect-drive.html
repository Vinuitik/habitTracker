Connect your Google Drive

Do you want to connect backups?

This app runs on a personal computer that isn't always online. If you connect your own Google Drive, the app can still work when the server is asleep — anything you do (mark a habit done, log a KPI value) gets queued through your own Drive and applied automatically the next time the server is up. Your data goes to your own Drive, encrypted, in a folder only this app can see — nobody else's account is involved.
